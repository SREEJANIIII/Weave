Final design

The Thread

One thread (Weave) runs over and under your apps, tying them into one day.

  • Three upright threads are your apps; the wavy thread is Weave, the one that connects them
  • Over-under gaps show Weave working with your apps, not on top of them
  • All black: timeless, prints anywhere, and the over-under gaps do all the work
