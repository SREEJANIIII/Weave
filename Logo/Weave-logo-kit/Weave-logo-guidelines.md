# Weave — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** three upright threads are your apps. The wavy thread is Weave: it runs over and under them and ties them into one day.
- **Versions:** horizontal (primary), stacked, symbol only, wordmark only.
- **Files:** `svg/` for websites, apps and print. `png/` for slides and documents. `web/` for favicon and app icons.

## 2. Clear space
Leave a clear zone around the logo equal to **one thread width**: the width of one upright bar in the symbol. Nothing (text, edges, other logos) goes inside it.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 96 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Stacked | 64 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol | 24 px (below this, use `weave-symbol-small.svg` or the favicon) | 6 mm |

## 4. Colour
The logo is **all black**. There is one colour and no accents.

| Name | Use | HEX | RGB | CMYK |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Black** | the logo on light backgrounds | #000000 | 0 0 0 | 0 0 0 100 |
| **White** | the logo on dark backgrounds and photos | #FFFFFF | 255 255 255 | 0 0 0 0 |

**Approved pairs:**
- black on white or any light colour
- white on black or any dark colour
- white on a calm area of a photo

Never use grey, colour or gradients in the logo itself.

## 5. Typography
- **Wordmark:** Inter SemiBold, lowercase, tight spacing, converted to outlines (never retype it).
- **App and website UI:** Inter (Regular / Medium / SemiBold). Fallback: system-ui, sans-serif.
- **Licence:** Inter is free under the SIL Open Font License, which allows logo use.

## 6. Don'ts
- Don't stretch, squash or rotate the logo.
- Don't recolour the logo or any one thread. It's always fully black or fully white.
- Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or glows.
- Don't close the small gaps where threads pass over and under. They are the idea.
- Don't rearrange the symbol and the wordmark, or change their size relative to each other.
- Don't place it on busy backgrounds without the black app-icon tile.

## 7. Notes
- The masters are clean filled outlines (no fonts, masks or effects), so they're safe for printers, laser-cutting and embroidery.
- Before a public launch, run a trademark search, and reverse-image-check the symbol with Google Lens or TinEye.
